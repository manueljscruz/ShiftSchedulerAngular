Absences

This section provides information about employee absences, including types of absences, policies, and procedures for reporting and managing time off.



Add Absence

First step to adding an absence is to click the "Add Absence" button present on the top part of the view.

After clicking the button, a form will appear where you can enter the details of the absence, such as the type of absence, start and end dates, and any additional notes.

Absences can be of type full day or partial. In a partial absence scenario, the user must specify the time intervals to which he will be absent.

Once all the necessary information has been filled out, click the "Create" button to add the absence to the system.

After saving, the new absence will appear in the absences grid.



Update and Delete

To update or delete absences, it is done by accessing the edit or delete buttons present on each row of their respective grids.

Pressing the edit button will open the same form as when creating an absence, but populated with the existing data.



Absence Types

When filling out the form, a mandatory absence type is required to be selected, so that it provides full context to the reason why. Here's a list of all available absence types:

  • Vacation — Planned time off approved in advance, used for rest, travel, or personal leisure.
  • Personal — Short-term leave for attending to personal matters that cannot be handled outside working hours.
  • Parental — Leave taken for the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, including time to support early caregiving.
  • Sabbatical — Extended leave granted for personal growth, research, travel, or rejuvenation, typically based on tenure or company policy.
  • Educational — Approved leave to pursue training, coursework, or professional development programs that support career growth.
  • Sick — Time off due to illness, injury, or medical appointments that prevent an employee from performing their duties.
  • Family Emergency — Unplanned absence required to address urgent or unexpected situations affecting immediate family members.
  • Bereavement — Leave taken in the event of the death of a family member to allow for grieving, funeral arrangements, or related matters.
  • Jury Duty — Legally mandated absence to serve as a juror or participate in related court proceedings.
  • Military — Leave required for military service, training, or related obligations as mandated by law.
  • Unpaid — Approved absence taken without compensation when other leave types are unavailable or not applicable.
  • Unauthorized Absence — Time away from work without prior approval or valid justification, not in compliance with company policy.


Absence Approval

Absences can only be approved by the owner or any other manager responsible for this section. This operation can be done in the absences grid status column with the thumb icons.

Clicking the thumbs up icon will approve the absence, while clicking the thumbs down icon will reject it. If approved, this absence will be taken into account when forming schedules, as the worker may or may not be available to specific shift entries.
