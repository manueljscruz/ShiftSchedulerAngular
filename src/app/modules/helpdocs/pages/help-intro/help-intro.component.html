Introduction

Welcome to the Shift Scheduler Help Center!
Shift Scheduler is a powerful and easy-to-use application designed to help teams create, manage, and maintain work schedules with efficiency and accuracy.

Whether you're an administrator creating schedules for your organization, a manager overseeing shifts, or a team member checking your assigned hours, this guide will help you get the most out of Shift Scheduler.



What is Shift Scheduler?

Shift Scheduler is a scheduling platform built to automate and simplify the creation of work schedules. It helps organizations eliminate manual planning, ensure fair distribution of workloads, and maintain visibility over member availability, absences, and work rules.



Who is it for?

Shift Scheduler is designed for:

  • Administrators who configure rules, create schedules, and manage members
  • Managers who oversee shifts, staffing, and team availability
  • Team Members who need to view assigned shifts, request changes, or track schedules


What can you do with Shift Scheduler

With Shift Scheduler, you can easily:

  • Create weekly, monthly, or custom schedules
  • Manage member profiles, roles, and availability
  • Define shift templates, scheduling rules, and constraints
  • Track absences and conflicts automatically
  • Review, publish, and share schedules
  • Configure notifications for changes or approvals
  • Manage subscription, billing, and account settings

Navigating Shift Scheduler

Shift Scheduler organizes its tools into a few core sections:

  • Members — Manage your team
  • Schedules — Create, publish, and review schedules
  • Shifts — Set up and maintain shift templates
  • Rules — Configure scheduling constraints and logic
  • Absences — Track member time off or exceptions
  • Profile & Account Settings — Manage your personal and organization details
