Members

This is where you'll see and manage the members of the organization.



List or Grid?

From this page, you can see all members and can choose in what you

  • List View — See members in a detailed list format, showing key information at a glance.
  • Grid View — View members as cards in a grid layout, providing a more visual representation.


Filter what you want to see

You can filter members by their roles, availability, or other criteria to quickly find specific individuals or groups within your organization.

By clicking on the filters button, a popup will appear with following options:

  • Name — Filter members based on their name;
  • Order — You can order alphabeticalically or by their seniority of the members, in other words, who have been part of the organization the longest;
  • Skill — Select one or more skills to filter members who have that skill assigned;
  • Assignments — Check for any member who does only specific shifts;
  • Availability — Filter members based on their availability, whether they are part of the rotation, work only during the week or weekends;


Adding New Members

To add a new member to the organization, click on the "Add Member" button. A popup will appear to which you have the following options:

  • Send Invite — Send an invitation to a potential new member's email address.
  • Create Profile — Manually create a profile to impersonate as a new member

When a member is invited by email, it will send a notification to the user to join your work organization, to which it will be up to decision. On the other hand, creating a profile will create an impersonator/standin that the manager can use for either simulation purposes or to take the place of someone who hasn't joined the work organization yet. This profile will be exclusive to the organization, so it will not be showned anywhere else.

  • Skills — Assign relevant skills to the member;
  • Part of Rotation — Indicates that the member will be assigned to the shifts based on the rotation specified by the manager;
  • Specific Shifts — Assign specific shifts for the user to work;
  • Multiple Shifts Assignments — Can be assigned to more than one shift a day;
  • Works Weekdays/Weekends — Determines the member's availability, during weekdays and weekends;

You can make these operations for when you wish to invite someone, or creating someone, to the exception of assigning specific shifts. Once the person has joined the work entity, you can then assign specific shifts if required.



Editing Members

As a manager, you can update these at any time, by pressing the edit button visible for each worker, in the "Actions" column. You can also remove them from the organization, using the remove button also present in the same column.
